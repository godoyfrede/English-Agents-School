# CLT Techniques Reference — Conversation Practice

## Core CLT Principles

1. **Communicative Competence over Linguistic Accuracy** — The goal is effective communication, not perfection
2. **Authentic Materials** — Use real-world language, not textbook dialogues
3. **Learner-Centered** — The student does most of the talking; the teacher guides
4. **Meaningful Interaction** — Every activity has a real communicative purpose
5. **Error Correction Strategy** — Focus on errors that block communication; note fluency errors for feedback later

## Interaction Patterns

### Information Gap Activities
One person has information the other needs. Forces real communication.
- Example: "I'll describe a picture. You tell me what's different in yours."
- Example: "I have half the schedule. You have the other half. Let's compare."

### Jigsaw Activities
Each student has a piece of the puzzle. Together they complete the picture.
- Example: "I read about the problem. You read about the solution. Let's discuss."

### Opinion Polls
Students express and defend opinions.
- Example: "Do you agree that technology is making us less social? Tell me why."

## Error Correction Strategies

### Recasting (Preferred — doesn't interrupt flow)
Student says: "Yesterday I go to the store."
You respond: "Oh, you *went* to the store! What did you buy?"
→ You repeat the correct form naturally without explicitly correcting

### Elicitation
Student says: "She don't like it."
You say: "She... ?" (pause, expectant look)
→ Prompt the student to self-correct

### Explicit Correction (Use sparingly, for repeated errors)
"That's almost right — we say 'doesn't like', not 'don't like'. Try again!"

### Feedback Sandwich
"Great vocabulary choice! Just remember: it's 'have gone', not 'have went'. But your idea was expressed really clearly!"

## Vocabulary Expansion Techniques

### Contextual Introduction
Never introduce words in isolation. Always in a sentence:
❌ "Postpone means to delay."
✅ "When you postpone a meeting, you decide to have it at a later time. For example: 'We postponed the trip because of the weather.'"

### Collocations (B2+)
Teach words in natural pairings:
- "Make a decision" (not "do a decision")
- "Take responsibility" (not "get responsibility")
- "Reach a compromise" (not "arrive a compromise")

### Idioms by Frequency
**Most common first (B1+):**
- "Break a leg" — Good luck!
- "Under the weather" — Feeling sick
- "Hit the nail on the head" — Exactly right
- "Piece of cake" — Very easy
- "Let the cat out of the bag" — Reveal a secret

## Pronunciation Focus Points

### Common Brazilian Portuguese Speaker Challenges
- Final consonants: "las**t**", "fas**t**", "lis**t**" (Brazilians often drop them)
- TH sounds: "**th**ink" vs "**t**ink" — tongue between teeth
- Schwa /ə/: the most common English vowel — "**a**bout", "syst**e**m"
- Word stress: "PHOtograph" vs "phoTOgrapher" vs "photoGRAPHic"
- False cognates: "actually" ≠ "atualmente" (it means "in fact/really")

### Minimal Pairs to Practice
- ship / sheep
- hit / heat
- pull / pool
- full / fool
- live / leave
